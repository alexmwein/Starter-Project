Talk track: “This campaign worked commercially. Fifty selected creators reached 11.4 million people, generated 328,000 engagements, and the featured dress sold out. Lord & Taylor even pre-approved every post. The FTC still brought charges because, according to its complaint, the agreements did not require disclosure of the paid and gifted relationships, and none of the approved posts included it.”

Lord & Taylor settled without admitting the complaint’s substantive allegations. The final order required creator instruction, signed acknowledgment, monitoring, and enforcement for 20 years. Do not imply a fine, creator misconduct, or commercial failure. The point is that creative approval alone is not an operating system.
