The working brief

Four decisions.
Then a shortlist.

01 · Business objective
What must this campaign change?
02 · Target consumer
Whose attention and action matter most?
03 · Required output
What must ship, where, and with what rights?
04 · Launch reality
What are the timing, budget, and approval constraints?

Turn this into live discovery. These four answers are enough to return with a reasoned first shortlist and scoped campaign direction.

Investment is scoped from creator mix, deliverables, production, usage, paid rights, and timing. Quote after these decisions are clear.
