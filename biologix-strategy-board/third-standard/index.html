The problem

A certificate is a claim. Documentation is a defense.

Most research materials arrive with a PDF and an assumption of trust. When a method is challenged, the assumption is what fails.

Third Standard is being designed around the record a reviewer actually needs: the named lot, its specification, the method behind each result, the chain of handling, the release decision, and every exception that changed the story.

The product

Anatomy of a Lot Record.

A single certificate cannot answer every quality question. The Lot Record keeps each evidence dimension separate, names its source, and makes the boundary visible.

Read the complete schema →
TS–LR / Record anatomy Schema preview — not a certificate or available lot.
Identity Named material, reference identifier, method, laboratory, report. A purity result does not establish identity by itself.
Composition Purity profile, related substances, method, acceptance boundary. The method and its limits belong beside the percentage.
Quantity Measured mass or content, uncertainty, unit, sample basis. Label quantity and measured quantity are different facts.
Handling Receipt, storage, custody events, release, shipment mapping. Evidence loses force when the tested sample cannot be tied to stock.
Exceptions Deviation, investigation, disposition, withdrawal or recall state. A defensible record includes the facts that went wrong.
