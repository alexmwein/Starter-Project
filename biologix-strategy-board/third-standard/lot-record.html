The evidence boundary.

Every field should answer two questions: what does this establish, and what does it not establish?

A composition report does not establish identity, quantity, custody, stability, or suitability for a particular application. The interface keeps those questions visibly separate so that confidence cannot silently spread beyond the underlying evidence.
