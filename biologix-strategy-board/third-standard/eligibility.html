Access boundary

Who we onboard.

Published criteria for research access. If you do not meet them yet, we say so plainly.

Check your institution ↓

  1. A verifiable research entity.

    A biotechnology company, CRO, university-affiliated laboratory, independent analytical lab, or comparable institutional research organization.

  2. An accountable person.

    A named applicant using an institutional domain, with a role connected to research, quality, procurement, or authorized operations.

  3. A real facility and context.

    A verifiable research setting and a specific analytical, in-vitro, or preclinical purpose that makes sense for the requested material and quantity.

  4. Purchasing authority.

    The applicant can authorize, recommend, or formally support procurement for the entity and can provide additional records when required.

  5. Use within the published boundary.

    No personal, household, clinical, therapeutic, veterinary, cosmetic, lifestyle, or resale purpose. No dosing, preparation, or administration support.

  6. Ongoing review.

    Access can be narrowed, paused, or withdrawn when the organization, use case, quantity, communication, or evidence no longer fits the approved scope.

A checkbox is not qualification.

The decision should consider the entity, person, facility, purpose, material, quantity, communication history, and available records together. A representation is evidence to review, not a shield from the surrounding facts.
