The material is only one part of the purchase.

Third Standard is being built for small research teams that need the procurement record to be as legible as the specification. The initial business combines controlled-access reference materials, custom procurement, independent verification coordination, and a Lot Record documentation layer.

The durable company is the record: approved accounts, specification history, supplier performance, custody, exceptions, and continuity.

Claims policy

What we may sayOnly what a current, named source establishes within its documented boundary.
What we will not say“Safe,” “medical grade,” “pharmaceutical grade,” “sterile,” “verified,” or “every batch tested” without exact current evidence and scope.
What we will not fabricateCustomers, reviews, inventory, certificates, lot IDs, test results, laboratory relationships, service levels, or operating history.
What the site is todayA pre-launch company and product concept. No material is offered for sale, no application is submitted, and no access is granted.
What must happen nextLegal review, supplier diligence, quality agreements, independent testing, insurance, transparent banking and payments, fulfillment validation, and a passed recall simulation.

Official channels

Permission before scale.
