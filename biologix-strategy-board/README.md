# Biologix strategy board

An internal three-view decision system for evaluating and executing the Biologix opportunity without letting it become an ungated distraction.

Open `index.html` directly or serve this directory over HTTP. All navigation, filters, expansion controls, and text layout work without a backend.

## Views

1. **Decision map** — why this may be a leverage shift rather than shiny-object syndrome, what Alex uniquely brings, and the gated sequence.
2. **Operating reference** — the paid sprint, 90-day roadmap, role, economics, NewCo boundary, reveal policy, proof gates, and walk-away conditions.
3. **Execution** — the next 72 hours and two action paths: Braden replies or Braden remains silent.

## Evidence standard

Every claim is labeled as one of:

- Verified public signal
- Founder-reported, unverified
- Strategic inference
- Required proof

All revenue, margin, ownership, inventory, and operating claims attributed to Biologix remain unverified until source records reconcile.
