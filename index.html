The two-human rule

Every signed creator has a primary Talent Manager and a named backup before activation. Both meet the creator during onboarding. The relationship, history, agreement, and inbox belong to OVO—not to either person.

Creator coverage protocol

01 / Accept

02 / Assign
Primary + backup

03 / Meet

30-day backup check

Zero-loss reassignment

The claim-leasing engine

The scraper creates supply. A manual, logged DM earns a temporary claim. Continued ownership is earned by completing the correct next action—not by hoarding records or spamming daily follow-ups.

Pool
Qualified + claimable

Scraper score, duplicate check, and no active owner.

Proof
Manual DM sent

MetaGraph/Chatwoot send event creates the claim and attribution.

Lease
Next action required

D0, D2, D5, and D10 cadence by default; stage can override.

Lock
Positive reply

Two-hour response SLA and a seven-day renewable relationship lock.

Recycle
Overdue → claimable

No completed next action by expiry returns the record to the pool with history intact.

Capacity
Start with a maximum of 25 open prospect claims per trainee.
No duplicates
One active claim per creator across every account and channel.
No private asset
Every send, reply, note, and call outcome synchronizes to the OVO record.

Routing order
