# OVO Organization and Creator Claim System

Status: supporting playbook under [SYSTEM.md](./SYSTEM.md)

Version: 1.0

Effective: 2026-08-21

## Operating model

OVO is a non-exclusive creator partnership network with centralized brand sales, account direction, campaign operations, and owned creator monetization.

The company runs on four rules:

1. OVO holds the canonical operating record, attribution, agreements, and history created through its systems; creators remain non-exclusive and retain their identity and independent relationships.
2. Every person owns one primary outcome, even when one person temporarily wears two seats.
3. Every signed creator has a primary Talent Manager and a named backup.
4. Prospect ownership is leased by completed work. It is never permanently acquired by clicking “claim.”

Crossing tracks requires the other track's certification. Strong performance in one role does not automatically predict performance in another.

## Creator relationship ownership

### Two-human rule

The CRM blocks creator activation until all of the following exist:

- Executed non-exclusive agreement
- Primary Talent Manager
- Backup Talent Manager or Creator Pod Lead
- Three-way onboarding completed
- OVO email alias and contact routing verified
- Full creator profile completed
- Next action and due date recorded

The primary and backup both meet the creator during onboarding. The backup performs an independent relationship-health check by day 30. If the primary leaves, the backup becomes primary and a new backup is assigned.

Patricia should not perform monthly calls with every creator. That would recreate a human routing bottleneck. Pod Leads provide institutional coverage. Patricia joins creator conversations only when a major brand-account outcome requires it.

### Required creator record

- Normalized identity and social handles
- Contact methods
- Niche, geography, audience, and source
- Primary manager, backup manager, Pod Lead
- Assignment and transfer history
- Rates, availability, exclusions, and brand fit
- Agreement and non-exclusive status
- OVO email alias
- Complete MetaGraph and Chatwoot conversation history
- Last completed action, next action, and due date
- Campaign participation and delivery performance
- Creator payouts, OVO contribution, and manager commission
- Relationship-health checks and departure reason
- InnerDM qualification and status

## Prospect claim leasing

### Principle

A scraper record is company inventory. A Talent Manager earns a temporary claim by sending a real, logged manual DM. Continued ownership is earned by completing the stage's required next action.

### States

| State | Entry evidence | Ownership rule |
|---|---|---|
| Claimable | Scraper qualification and duplicate checks pass | No active owner |
| Claimed | Manual send event exists in MetaGraph or Chatwoot | Temporary lease begins |
| Awaiting reply | Required follow-up date exists | Lease survives while actions are current |
| Engaged | Creator replies | Owner has a two-business-hour response SLA |
| Qualified | Commercial and relationship criteria pass | Manager keeps priority |
| Signed | Non-exclusive agreement executed | Permanent primary and backup required |
| Activated | Alias, profile, payment, and onboarding complete | Creator enters active portfolio |
| Recycled | Required action expires | Record returns to claimable with history intact |

### Initial timers

- Default outreach cadence: day 0, day 2, day 5, and day 10
- Positive reply response SLA: two business hours
- Engaged-record lock: seven days, renewed by completed next actions
- Overdue warning: immediately when the next action passes
- Automatic recycle: after the configured grace period, initially 24 hours
- Initial claim capacity: maximum 25 open prospect claims per trainee

Daily forced follow-ups are prohibited unless the creator explicitly expects them. They incentivize spam and create platform risk. The system enforces the right action for the stage, not meaningless daily activity.

### Claim controls

- One active claim per creator across every OVO account and channel
- Normalized-handle, phone, and email duplicate checks
- Claim creation requires provider send evidence
- Every message and call outcome synchronizes to the shared record
- A manager cannot claim above their open-record capacity
- Managers cannot hide or delete shared contact history
- Reassignment preserves original source and every previous owner
- Signed creators cannot return to the prospect pool

## Large brand workflow

1. Brand SDR or AE creates qualified demand through calls, email, LinkedIn, or strategic creator-alias inbound.
2. Kennedy closes the opportunity after the executed-agreement and activation-payment gate, then completes the commercial handoff packet.
3. Patricia accepts or rejects the packet within one business day. On acceptance, she becomes the brand account's accountable owner.
4. Jaci creates the campaign plan, creator requirements, delivery calendar, margin plan, and 20% staffing buffer.
5. The system identifies matching creators and their primary Talent Managers.
6. Each Talent Manager accepts or declines the request within 24 hours and confirms creator participation.
7. Jaci monitors campaign milestones and exceptions.
8. Talent Managers manage creator-side commitments.
9. Patricia communicates consolidated status to the brand.
10. Renewal or material expansion opens a new opportunity. Patricia originates it from the retained account; Kennedy re-enters when closing work is required.

## Failure handling

For a Talent Manager who misses required obligations:

1. First miss: written warning and corrective commitment.
2. Second miss within 30 days: reduced portfolio and probation.
3. Third miss: portfolio reassigned and seat removed.

Commission eligibility requires collected cash, successful fulfillment, reconciled costs, valid attribution, and the manager remaining in good standing through the earning event.

## AI communication monitoring

AI may:

- Extract commitments and deadlines
- Summarize communication
- Detect missing replies
- Flag negative sentiment
- Compare messages with campaign obligations
- Create risk alerts

AI may not independently promise pricing, commercial terms, creator acceptance, deadlines, or exceptions. The campaign board remains the system of record. Any capture or analysis of private communication must use properly disclosed, reviewed data handling.
