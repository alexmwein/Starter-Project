# OVO Operating System

This folder defines how OVO turns brand demand and creator relationships into collected contribution without making Alex the router for routine work.

Start with [SYSTEM.md](./SYSTEM.md). It is the canonical operating contract. If another file or the command-center prototype disagrees with it, `SYSTEM.md` wins.

## Current model

OVO is one company with two commercial engines and one shared capability layer:

1. **Brand Partnerships** creates near-term cash and recurring demand.
2. **InnerDM** is the owned product bet and earns scale through retained, contribution-positive cohorts.
3. **Creator Network + Academy** supplies trusted creators and trained operators to real funded work. It is not an independent growth mandate during the current proof cycle.

The company objective is:

> Increase monthly contribution from retained creator relationships while reducing founder routing hours.

## Five operating pipelines

The system deliberately separates objects that used to be mixed together:

1. Brand opportunities
2. Campaigns
3. Creator relationships
4. InnerDM cohorts
5. Operator capacity

A durable brand account is not an opportunity. A closed opportunity is not a completed campaign. An invoice is not collected cash. A renewal is a new opportunity linked to the retained account.

## Current proof-cycle gates

### Brand Partnerships

- Run the anchor account profitably without Alex in routine fulfillment.
- Collect one additional recurring brand using the standard offer and handoff.
- Keep delivery at or above 95% on time and contribution at or above the approved floor.

### InnerDM

- Launch five qualified creators under one playbook and executed terms.
- Scale only if at least three are retained and contribution-positive at D60.
- Keep ongoing direct labor below four hours per retained creator per month.

### Creator Network + Academy

- Open operator seats only against funded capacity.
- Require contribution-positive output inside 30 days and two productive months for retention.
- Keep Academy internal until at least three graduates produce collected contribution for two consecutive months.

### Founder dependency

- Keep Alex below five hours per week of routine routing for two consecutive weeks.
- Convert repeated interruptions into a rule, assignment, checklist, automation, or stopped activity.
- Start no new business line during the proof cycle.

## Data honesty

The command center is a local planning prototype, not a live system of record. A current metric is valid only when it has a source, as-of date, and freshness limit. Missing or expired evidence displays `Unknown` or `Stale`; old baselines never turn green merely because they exist in browser storage.

## Definition of installed

The system is installed when:

- every active object has a canonical record, accountable seat, state, next action, due time, economics, and evidence;
- ten records pass each relevant handoff without Alex routing them;
- all scorecard values reconcile to a named source;
- at least 90% of routine decisions are made by the named seat; and
- founder routine-operations time stays below five hours for two consecutive weeks.
