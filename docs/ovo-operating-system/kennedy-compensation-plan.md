## 1. Compensation headline

| Component | Annual amount at target | Monthly equivalent |
|---|---:|---:|
| Guaranteed base salary | $45,000 | $3,750 |
| Target variable compensation | $105,000 | $8,750 |
| On-target earnings (OTE) | $150,000 | $12,500 |

The base salary is paid through payroll on the Company's regular pay cycle. OTE is not guaranteed compensation. Variable compensation is uncapped and is earned only under the rules below.

The $45,000 base compensates Kennedy for the ongoing non-sales duties of operating Academy recruiting, managing students, maintaining the CRM, running candidate and student follow-up, producing recruiting content, managing approved sales representatives, and reporting Academy performance.

## 2. Net Collected Revenue

All percentage commissions are calculated only on **Net Collected Revenue**.

"Net Collected Revenue" means tuition cash actually received and settled in the Company's account, less taxes, refunds, chargebacks, credits, discounts, payment reversals, financing costs, and payment-processing fees.

- A signed agreement, invoice, deposit promise, payment plan, or CRM stage does not create commission eligibility.
- For installment plans, commission is earned separately as each installment settles.
- Refunded or charged-back cash reverses the related commission and is offset against the next variable-compensation statement to the extent permitted by applicable law.
- Unauthorized discounts, pricing exceptions, or deals below the approved contribution-margin floor are not commissionable unless approved in writing before collection.

## 3. Paid Academy seat commissions

Only one of the following rates applies to a particular dollar of Net Collected Revenue. Rates do not stack.

| Kennedy's attributable role | Rate |
|---|---:|
| Kennedy personally sources and personally closes the paid seat | 15% |
| Kennedy personally closes a Company- or team-sourced lead | 10% |
| Kennedy personally sources the lead and another approved closer closes it | 5% |
| An approved sales representative Kennedy directly recruited, trained, and actively manages closes it | 3% management override |

For a transaction where more than one rate could apply, Kennedy receives the single highest applicable rate. No commission is payable twice for sourcing, closing, and management on the same revenue.

The management override applies only while Kennedy is the documented active manager of the representative and only to paid Academy tuition. It creates no permanent or multilevel downline right and does not apply to brand revenue, creator revenue, InnerDM revenue, or other OVO business lines without a separate written addendum.

## 4. Sponsored-seat productivity incentive

Sponsored Academy seats do not generate a sales commission because no tuition cash is collected. Kennedy may earn up to **$1,500 per approved sponsored representative**, but only after that representative produces collected contribution for OVO.

| Collected outcome from the sponsored representative | Kennedy's earned incentive |
|---|---:|
| First $2,000 in cumulative collected contribution | $500 |
| $5,000 in cumulative collected contribution and still active | $500 |
| $10,000 in cumulative collected contribution and productive at day 90 | $500 |

Certification, attendance, interviews, acceptance, signatures, assigned seats, or creator count alone do not earn compensation. A representative must be approved against available profitable inventory before entering the sponsored program.

## 5. Student-success quality bonus

Kennedy may earn up to **$3,750 per quarter**, or **$15,000 annually at target**, for operating Academy with acceptable student outcomes.

The quarterly bonus is available only if:

1. Academy has positive contribution for the quarter after commissions, refunds, and direct delivery costs; and
2. The quarterly Net Collected Revenue target has been met.

If both gates are met, the bonus is scored equally across these four measures:

| Quarterly quality measure | Target | Bonus weight |
|---|---:|---:|
| Paid-student completion or objective certification | At least 80% | 25% |
| Refund and chargeback rate | Below 5% | 25% |
| Documented student-support SLA attainment | At least 95% | 25% |
| Sponsored representatives productive at day 60 | At least 60% | 25% |

Each satisfied measure earns 25% of the quarterly bonus. Failed or undocumented measures earn zero for that portion. Certification standards may not be waived to increase bonus attainment.

## 7. Accelerators and uncapped upside

Accelerators apply only to incremental Net Collected Revenue above the applicable annual cash quota; they are not retroactive.

| Annual quota attainment | Multiplier on the applicable percentage rate |
|---|---:|
| 0%–100% | 1.00x |
| Above 100% through 125% | 1.20x |
| Above 125% | 1.35x |

Examples:

- A 15% personally sourced-and-closed rate becomes 18% on incremental cash above 100% of that quota and 20.25% above 125%.
- A 3% management override becomes 3.6% and then 4.05% on incremental team cash at the corresponding attainment levels.

There is no commission cap, but all cash must remain properly attributed, collected, refund-cleared, and above the approved margin floor.

## 8. Attribution

- The source, owner, closer, payment amount, collection date, and representative manager must be recorded in the approved CRM.
- A sourced lead must be logged before another person or channel records the same lead.
- Source attribution lasts 90 days from the last documented substantive activity by Kennedy unless reassigned in writing.
- A management override requires the representative to be assigned to Kennedy before the qualifying sales activity.
- Patricia or the designated finance owner reconciles commission eligibility to settled cash. Kennedy may review and dispute a statement within 30 days.

## 9. Payment timing

- Finance closes the prior month's cash and refund reconciliation after month-end.
- Earned commissions are paid on the next regular payroll after the applicable refund window and reconciliation are complete.
- Sponsored-representative incentives are paid after the applicable collected-contribution threshold is verified.
- Quarterly quality bonuses are paid after the quarterly contribution and quality metrics are finalized.
- OVO provides a variable-compensation statement showing the cash, rate, reversals, and resulting payment.

## 10. Ramp and review

- Month 1 performance expectation: 25% of full monthly production target.
- Month 2: 50%.
- Month 3: 75%.
- Month 4 onward: 100%.
- Commission rates remain unchanged during ramp; the reduced targets govern performance review only.
- The parties review pricing, quota attainability, contribution margin, and attribution quality after 90 days.
- Changes apply prospectively and require a written plan amendment; OVO does not retroactively reduce already-earned commissions.
