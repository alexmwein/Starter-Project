# Third Standard: Company Thesis

## The current concept

Third Standard is a clearly fictional peptide-company website. It demonstrates how an evidence-first research catalog could work without pretending that a business, product, test program, or supply chain already exists.

Today, Third Standard has:

- no inventory or physical material;
- no released lots or specifications;
- no laboratory reports or certificates;
- no customers, orders, checkout, payment, or fulfillment;
- no application submission or account system;
- no offer to sell anything.

A concise notice at the top of every public page states:

> Fictional concept. Nothing described here exists, is stocked, or is offered for sale.

The rest of the experience is deliberately specific. It presents eight named peptide dossiers, a proposed Lot Record architecture, scientific notes, and a browser-only research profile flow. Specificity makes the concept useful. Empty evidence states keep it honest.

The conceptual customer promise is:

> Peptides you can defend.

This is not a consumer peptide storefront with restrained typography. It demonstrates the opposite operating model:

- evidence status before promotional claims;
- a deliberately narrow peptide index before catalog breadth;
- clear boundaries between identity, specification, characterization, release, and availability;
- lot records before generic COAs;
- professional research context before any future commercial flow;
- no dosing, administration, preparation, transformation, or human-use content;
- no lifestyle affiliates or “research subject” euphemisms.

No dossier may show a price, stock quantity, sale badge, cart action, quote request, waitlist, delivery promise, fabricated COA, invented test result, or implied availability.

## The primary journeys

The website has four intended journeys:

1. **Browse peptides.** Use the named index to understand the catalog taxonomy and open a dossier.
2. **Inspect the Lot Record.** See the information architecture a future released lot would require. The current page is a schema demonstration, not a real record.
3. **Read notes.** Learn how specifications, test methods, characterization, and evidence boundaries differ.
4. **Prepare a local research profile.** Enter institutional context in a browser-only flow. The data stays local and does not create an account, contact a company, or begin a transaction.

These journeys make the site useful before any commercial infrastructure exists.

## Why the category matters

The underlying peptide and metabolic-research market is durable. Eli Lilly reported that Mounjaro and Zepbound represented 56% of its 2025 revenue, while Novo Nordisk reported Wegovy sales of DKK 79.1 billion in 2025. That validates extraordinary scientific and commercial attention around peptide therapeutics.

The open consumer “research-use-only” storefront is not the durable layer. FDA’s March 31, 2026 Gram Peptides warning states that research-only labels did not overcome human-use evidence on the site, including body-effect claims and bacteriostatic water sold beside peptides. FDA separately states that retatrutide cannot be used in compounding under federal law and has not been found safe and effective for any condition.

Third Standard therefore does not demonstrate a cleverer version of that storefront. It explores a more legible scientific information and procurement model while keeping the current website fictional and non-commercial.

## What the competitor work taught us

The best observed peptide companies each own one strong layer:

- American Peptides: clear product hierarchy, strong on-page conversion, and visible documentation.
- Modern Peptides: current, upcoming, and historical COA states placed close to products.
- Protide: a simple lot-lookup concept and predictable trust architecture.
- Pure U.S. Peptide: deep product dossiers, citations, and a strong content surface.
- Skye Peptides: controlled access and an account-first gate.
- Loti Labs: broad catalog depth and frequent educational content.
- Core Peptides and Peptide Sciences: catalog and search authority.

Large scientific suppliers add other useful patterns:

- dense but predictable product-detail pages;
- scientific taxonomy instead of lifestyle merchandising;
- clear separation between specification and batch evidence;
- document retrieval as a primary task;
- literature and method context adjacent to product identity.

Third Standard combines the strongest patterns into a quieter sequence:

`peptide index → dossier → evidence status → Lot Record → notes → local research profile`

The concept rejects conversion patterns that would imply commerce, availability, human outcomes, or evidence that does not exist.

## The future business hypothesis

If Third Standard ever moves beyond a website concept, the possible wedge remains:

1. Analytical reference materials for qualified research teams.
2. Custom synthesis brokerage and project management.
3. Independent verification coordination and evidence packaging.
4. Lot Record software for laboratories and research suppliers.

These are hypotheses, not current products or services. No inventory should be purchased and no commerce should launch until a specialized FDA/FDCA attorney, customs counsel, insurer, bank, processor, testing partner, and qualified fulfillment partner approve the exact material list and operating flow in writing.

## The potential moat

The public website is not the moat. A future moat could compound through:

- approved institutional accounts;
- specification and reorder history;
- lot-to-document-to-customer traceability;
- vendor performance data;
- exception and complaint records;
- documentation workflow;
- responsive procurement support;
- a trusted scientific publication;
- the Lot Record software layer.

None of those assets exists in the current concept. The present value is the information architecture and positioning.

## Possible future company architecture

### Third Standard Supply Co.

Could own qualified institutional commerce, inventory contracts, fulfillment agreements, and customer support after all launch gates are cleared.

### Third Standard Records

Could own the documentation schema, Lot Record software, vendor scorecards, and evidence delivery.

### Licensed-care partnerships

If a future audience includes individuals seeking medical outcomes, route them away from the research catalog and toward separately contracted, licensed providers. Third Standard should not prescribe, compound, dispense, or make medical claims.

These entities and relationships are conceptual. They are not current operations.

## Hard stop rules

- No ordinary consumer checkout.
- No false purchaser attestations.
- No product page that explains human outcomes.
- No reconstitution kits, syringes, injection supplies, or bacteriostatic water.
- No creator compensation tied to consumer product sales.
- No bank or processor application that omits the exact catalog and model.
- No overseas supplier selected from a marketplace listing alone.
- No “pharmaceutical grade,” “medical grade,” “safe,” or “sterile” claim without exact, current support.
- No concept evidence presented as real proof.
- No price, stock, order, quote, waitlist, account, or fulfillment control while nothing is operational.
- No Lot Record presented as a certificate for material that does not exist.

## Current objective

Build the most credible fictional peptide-company website in the category without crossing the line into fabricated commerce or evidence. The immediate success criteria are:

- the fiction notice is simple and impossible to miss;
- the eight peptide dossiers feel consistent, specific, and scientifically literate;
- the Lot Record makes documentation boundaries obvious;
- the notes build trust through useful research literacy;
- the local profile flow demonstrates qualification without transmitting data;
- no page implies that material, inventory, tests, lots, customers, or company operations exist.

If the concept is ever validated and cleared for real operation, the future objective is to prove that qualified laboratory accounts will pay a premium for legible evidence and procurement continuity, then convert the workflow into software and services that remain useful regardless of any single molecule or regulatory cycle.
