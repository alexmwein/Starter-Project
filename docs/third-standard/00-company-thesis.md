# Third Standard — Company Thesis

## The company

Third Standard is a controlled-access research supply and documentation company. Its wedge is peptide reference materials for qualified laboratories; its durable asset is the lot-level evidence and procurement record attached to every material.

The customer promise is:

> Research materials you can defend.

This is not a consumer peptide store with better typography. It is the opposite operating model:

- qualification before transaction;
- a deliberately narrow catalog before breadth;
- evidence boundaries before marketing claims;
- lot records before generic COAs;
- professional procurement support before anonymous checkout;
- no dosing, administration, preparation, transformation, or human-use content;
- no lifestyle affiliates or “research subject” euphemisms.

## Why now

The underlying peptide and metabolic-research market is durable. Eli Lilly reported that Mounjaro and Zepbound represented 56% of its 2025 revenue, while Novo Nordisk reported Wegovy sales of DKK 79.1 billion in 2025. That validates extraordinary scientific and commercial attention around peptide therapeutics.

The open consumer “research-use-only” storefront is not the durable layer. FDA’s March 31, 2026 Gram Peptides warning states that research-only labels did not overcome human-use evidence on the site, including body-effect claims and bacteriostatic water sold beside peptides. FDA separately states that retatrutide cannot be used in compounding under federal law and has not been found safe and effective for any condition.

Third Standard therefore does not compete to be the cleverest version of that storefront. It competes to be the most legible small-team research procurement experience.

## What the competitor work taught us

The best observed competitors each own one strong layer:

- American Peptides: clear pricing ladders, subscriptions, partner economics, and strong on-page conversion.
- Modern Peptides: deep product-adjacent COA history and points-based retention.
- Skye Peptides: controlled access and a public reputation for broader test panels.
- Loti Labs: content breadth and high-frequency search capture.
- Ion and similar operators: written creator-governance controls.
- Core and Peptide Sciences: catalog/search authority.

The market gap is the combination:

`qualification → specification review → lot evidence → procurement → shipment record → exception history → reorder continuity`

## The wedge

Launch with four revenue lines, in order:

1. Analytical reference materials for qualified research teams.
2. Custom synthesis brokerage and project management.
3. Independent verification coordination and evidence packaging.
4. Lot Record software for laboratories and research suppliers.

No inventory is purchased until a specialized FDA/FDCA attorney, customs counsel, insurer, bank, processor, testing partner, and qualified fulfillment partner approve the exact material list and operating flow in writing.

## The moat

The storefront is not the moat. The moat compounds through:

- approved institutional accounts;
- specification and reorder history;
- lot-to-document-to-customer traceability;
- vendor performance data;
- exception and complaint records;
- documentation workflow;
- responsive procurement support;
- a trusted research publication;
- the future Lot Record software layer.

## Company architecture

### Third Standard Supply Co.

Owns qualified research commerce, inventory contracts, fulfillment agreements, and customer support.

### Third Standard Records

Owns the documentation schema, Lot Record software, vendor scorecards, and evidence delivery.

### Licensed-care partnerships

If the audience includes individuals seeking medical outcomes, route them away from the research catalog and toward separately contracted, licensed providers. Third Standard does not prescribe, compound, dispense, or make medical claims.

## Hard stop rules

- No ordinary consumer checkout.
- No false purchaser attestations.
- No product page that explains human outcomes.
- No reconstitution kits, syringes, injection supplies, or bacteriostatic water.
- No creator compensation tied to consumer product sales.
- No bank or processor application that omits the exact catalog and model.
- No overseas supplier selected from a marketplace listing alone.
- No “pharmaceutical grade,” “medical grade,” “safe,” or “sterile” claim without exact, current support.
- No launch while the site contains concept evidence presented as real proof.

## Twelve-month objective

Prove that 25 qualified laboratory accounts will pay a premium for legible evidence and procurement continuity, then convert the workflow into software and services that remain useful regardless of any single molecule or regulatory cycle.
