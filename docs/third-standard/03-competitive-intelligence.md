# Third Standard — Competitive Intelligence

## Research basis

This synthesis combines the existing 18-brand and 30-seller market work in this repository with a fresh July 23, 2026 review of current public pages.

Current pages confirm:

- American Peptides offers explicit product pricing, subscriptions up to 15% off, quantity discounts up to 16%, adjacent preparation supplies, 13 visible reviews on the observed page, and an affiliate ladder up to 35%.
- Modern Peptides places 12 current and historical COAs directly on the observed GLP-3 pack page and uses points for retention.
- Skye Peptides redirects anonymous test-report access to login, reinforcing its qualified-access posture.
- FDA’s March 2026 Gram Peptides letter rejects the idea that “research use only” controls when the rest of the website indicates human use.
- FDA’s current GLP-1 page states that retatrutide cannot be compounded under federal law and has not been found safe and effective for any condition.

## Conversion patterns worth learning from

| Pattern | Why it converts | Third Standard adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| Price and quantity clarity | Reduces uncertainty | Publish quote logic and project minimums after qualification |
| Product-adjacent COAs | Proof appears at decision time | Put the Lot Record beside every available specification |
| Subscription/points | Creates retention | Use reserved-lot continuity and document alerts, not consumer gamification |
| Fast-shipping promise | Signals operational competence | Publish measured service levels only after pilot data |
| Affiliate dashboards | Scales distribution | Replace lifestyle affiliates with institutional referral and partner attribution |
| Broad catalog | Captures more demand | Begin narrow; expand only when evidence and operations can support it |
| Search education | Captures high-intent traffic | Own evidence literacy and research procurement |

## Patterns explicitly rejected

- Body-effect copy disguised as “research mechanisms.”
- Human-use reviews and transformation stories.
- Syringe, preparation, or injection cross-sells.
- Consumer subscription replenishment.
- Creators promoting research products to lifestyle audiences.
- Hidden product names, coded names, or euphemisms intended to evade review.
- A modal or checkbox used as the entire qualification system.
- “Every batch tested” without lot-specific, current, scoped evidence.

## Competitive position

Third Standard should not claim the cheapest material, biggest catalog, or highest purity.

It should own:

> The clearest evidence trail for small research teams.
