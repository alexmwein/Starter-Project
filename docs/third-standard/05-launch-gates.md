# Third Standard — Launch Gates

This document separates a polished concept from a company that has permission to transact.

## Gate A — Legal

- [ ] Exact entity formed and disclosed.
- [ ] Specialized counsel approves each launch material and claim.
- [ ] Importer-of-record and customs plan approved.
- [ ] Terms, privacy, qualification, returns, complaints, and recall policies reviewed.
- [ ] No product or marketing term implies human use.

## Gate B — Quality

- [ ] Supplier audit/diligence complete.
- [ ] Quality agreement signed.
- [ ] Release specifications approved.
- [ ] Independent laboratory contracts signed.
- [ ] Pilot lots tested and reviewed.
- [ ] Lot-to-customer traceability tested.
- [ ] Recall simulation passed.

## Gate C — Financial counterparties

- [ ] Bank has exact entity, owners, catalog, supplier geography, and expected volume.
- [ ] Processor has the same exact information.
- [ ] Insurers have the exact catalog and operating model.
- [ ] No application depends on a generic “e-commerce” or “supplements” description.

## Gate D — Operations

- [ ] Storage and fulfillment partner approved for exact materials.
- [ ] Inventory controls and cycle counts tested.
- [ ] Support and escalation coverage live.
- [ ] Deviation and complaint workflows tested.
- [ ] Order-to-cash reconciliation tested.

## Gate E — Product and website truth

- [ ] Every public claim has an evidence owner.
- [ ] Every real Lot Record maps to current inventory.
- [ ] Concept examples are removed or remain unmistakably labeled.
- [ ] Application and contact flows send data to an approved system.
- [ ] Analytics exclude sensitive research details and follow the privacy policy.
- [ ] Accessibility, mobile, performance, and security checks pass.

## Current status
